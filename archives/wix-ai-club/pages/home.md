## Welcome to AI Club
of IIT Madras!

### WHO WE ARE

We are a student community of Data Science and Machine Learning enthusiasts who explore various fields of AI and ML, solve challenging problems in the form of projects, solve real-world problems using state-of-art AI in various hackathons.

### WHAT WE DO

Work on tackling challenging real-world problems in diverse fields like Data Science, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Reinforcement Learning and more!

​

Conduct sessions and workshops on various topics and hackathons as well that keep us with the various advancements in tech.​​

​

The members of our Hackathon Vertical participate in various hackathons representing IITM. We also contribute to research, by publishing research papers and contributing to open source.​

### ACHIEVEMENTS

### Convolve 2023

Won the first edition of convolve pan IIT hackathon hosted at IIT Guwahati in collaboration with Cisco. Our team implemented a 98% accurate customer feedback sentiment analysis model using large transformer architectures.
